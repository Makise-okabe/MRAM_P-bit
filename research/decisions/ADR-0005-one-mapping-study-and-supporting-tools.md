# ADR-0005: One mapping study, a supporting predictor and conditional extensions

Date: 4 November 2026 (Singapore).
Status: active working organization clarified; final method, originality and supervisor approval remain unresolved.

## Context and correction

The user's supplied critique identifies an unrecorded change in presentation. The original discussion topics were reliability-aware mapping, inference-failure prediction and Ising. Later explanations elevated spatial-footprint read control into a replacement top-level candidate. That was a communication/scope error, not an evidence-based decision to eliminate the predictor.

Preserve the method-family IDs. This ADR clarifies roles; it does not introduce another candidate or establish a new algorithm's value.

## Working hierarchy

| Existing topic | Active role | Deliverable / gate |
|---|---|---|
| F3 reliability-aware mapping | Main bounded method investigation | One deployable resident mapper using a physically supported decoded-error structure, compared with strong tuned alternatives |
| F1 inference-failure predictor | Supporting scoring/evaluation tool inside the mapping study | A validated, affordable estimate used to rank/search mappings; independent task-level simulation checks finalists |
| F7 Ising | Optional diagnostic/alternative | Continue only after a specific implementable rule, relevant target operator and strong digital/periodic-refresh comparison exist |

Spatial matched-mask and interaction checks (H1/E02-A) are physical mechanism screens supporting the same neural study. A split-read controller is a conditional second lever within F3, not a new fourth project or an automatic chapter. Start from the static mapper. Add runtime control only if consequential residual effects survive practical correction and longer sensing, and only if the incremental benefit exceeds overhead.

Offline mapping and runtime scheduling have different metadata, routing and latency costs. Consolidating their scope does not make them equivalent algorithms or prove either original. If a controller is evaluated, isolate its incremental contribution rather than attributing its gain to mapping. A controller result cannot silently rescue a failed mapper novelty claim.

## What can run now

Its recorded packet/hash, ideal arithmetic checks, split identities and CAL-only maps are authoritative for workload evidence. Do not restart it merely because a previous instruction said 'run the pilot now'.

Legal work in parallel with E02: inspect workload activity/overlap, specify the mapper and predictor interfaces, implement/debug search on explicitly labelled assumed models, and prepare comparator budgets. No new electrical physics is needed to count skipped reads or verify permutations.

However, calibrated target error covariance, MRAM inference failure and a target-valid reliability-aware mapper comparison still require the new physical readout model. Reusing software/checkpoints/traces does not authorize fitting or evaluating the target with retired electrical tables. A lumped model without distributed resistance cannot establish a spatial mechanism.

## Current numerical evidence and forecast policy

The E01 audit reports CAR versus original-order prospective read reductions of 35.80%, 31.33% and 25.41% at 32/64/128-row groups. Against zero-rate sorting, the reductions are only 0.87%, 0.12% and approximately 0.025%. These are baseline workload counts, not energy, a new mapping gain, or a matched-failure comparison.

Therefore the supplied '10-30% fewer reads at the same failure rate' is not accepted as an evidence-backed forecast, confidence interval or promised benefit. A deliberately labelled engineering ambition could be set before final evaluation, with its baseline and quality budget stated. It may not be derived from constructed/retired-model outcomes as a prediction for the new array.

F3 may instead earn a quality improvement at equal read cost. A claim about cross-cycle covariance requires a physical mechanism breaking permutation invariance and evidence beyond conventional sorting/skipping.

## Predictor and final evaluation

Use the simplest adequate scorer initially. The reconstructed-MSE score is not itself a neural failure probability. If a failure predictor is used, validate its probability estimates and mapping rankings against independent paired noisy-network evaluations on a predeclared subset/finalists. Retain persistent chips across all reads, parent identities, signed coefficients and the real decoder.

Fit/tune only on CAL. Report original clean-reference disagreements and ground-truth classification errors separately on independent development/evaluation data, with uncertainty. Count calibration, fitting, offline search and inference costs. Predictor-guided optimization can exploit approximation errors, so final validation cannot use the predictor as its own ground truth. Do not create a separate unbounded estimator contribution by default.

## Physical and prior-art gates

Keep distributed WL/BL/SL RC and practical driver/sense loading in the fresh 256x256 foundation. Resistance-induced settled distortion, capacitance/settling and temporal stochastic noise are distinct. Longer common sensing is a baseline for finite-time effects; its latency/energy cost must be included.

Compete with tuned references/calibration, applicable SEC-style correction, activity-adaptive read policies, original and zero-rate order, CAR/balancing, fixed random, equal-offline-budget random search and applicable channel shuffling.

Princeton DAC 2022 Section 3.2 describes channel shuffling of partial computations across physical columns to randomize deterministic column errors. Do not describe this as a verified general static correction of row-position-dependent IR drop. DIB-CIM's publisher abstract establishes adaptive WL/input-block and ADC control; full-method comparison remains necessary.
