## Derivation and constructed check

Developed the [covariance-mapping candidate](../novelty/2026-11-04-covariance-mapping.md).
Its discriminating test holds read counts, per-read activity and marginal decoded-error
distributions equal. A four-group construction changes cross-cycle covariance after
scalar group calibration. Nonlinear decoding produces a reconstructed-MSE difference;
the continuous-readout control is invariant. The precise identity retains bias and pair
moments, which suffice for MSE but not a failure-probability distribution.

Before execution wrote the normalized illustrative configuration. E03M's first run uses
256x256 binary storage bookkeeping, no retired trial/PDK data, all-column analytic checks
and one-column paired Monte Carlo under three predeclared seeds. The analytic MSE
reduction is 26.97%; both conditional-mean and full-group gain calibration retained the
effect in sampling. The curated record contains uncertainty and hashes. This is not a
target circuit, workload, energy or inference result and is not an executed target E03.
