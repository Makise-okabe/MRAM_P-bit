## Best candidate research GAP, still OPEN
**Does actual extracted GF 22FDX local-floating 2T1MTJ parallel CIM exhibit *joint, position-structured* errors across columns/bitplane reads that a marginal-only physical error model fails to capture at full W4A8 network inference, and does a specific low-overhead computational policy exploit that nonseparability better than state-of-art matched-cost baselines?**

### Gates
1. **Target physical verification FIRST:** Recover actual 4×4 DSPF; prove parallel-read legality and readout interface. Then representative 16/32-row 2T1MTJ PEX or calibrated hierarchical model. Do not label E24 assumed-wire circuit as GF array extraction. Confirm real scale of interconnect and run case.
2. **Exact task:** freeze all W4A8 network layers/bias/mapping/bit-plane polarities; no fake classifier inference from FC1-only. Confirm hardware error distribution with correct signs.
3. **Falsify joint effect:** Compare matched **same per-bitplane/count marginal error rates**, but (A) independent shuffled errors versus (B) simultaneous physically modeled joint errors; evaluate MAC, logits and held-out classification flips. If no difference, covariance hypothesis fails.
4. **Only then propose an intervention** with formal objective and matched overhead versus fixed correction; signed bit significance, per-layer sensitivity, DIB-like input scheduling, Dyn-A-Map-like mapping, SEC-CiM-like selective protection, conventional retraining and simple Q correction. At equal error distributions and budget, *any* claimed advantage must survive all strong plausible comparisons.
5. A generic matrix covariance equation `Var(Δy)=c^T Σ c` is **not a new algorithm**. Without a new policy, physically plausible independent test or comparative gain, this remains a thesis concept, not a publication claim.
