\section{Exploratory five- and eight-bit extension on viewed M5 (EX1)}
\label{sec:ex1-m5-width}

\paragraph{Scope and immutable reference.}
Following E0's negative schedule-replacement finding,
the original 50,000-update linear anneal was retained.
EX1 tested \emph{only} FP5 (custom finite-only E2M2)
and signed INT8 at ideal response $d=0$ on the
previously viewed dense M5 graph
\texttt{ising2.5-100\_7777}. Each new format reused
all 24 historical M5 paired seeds, beta and
global source-only MSE scaling rule. The frozen
M5 FP64, FP6 and INT6 rows were reused
without rerunning the solver. Thus EX1 adds
exactly 48 new evaluations, not a fresh
held-out test or a new device population.

The user-supplied host execution reports
48/48 new runs complete and strict M5 archive
checks pass. At initial transcription, the
new \texttt{new\_runs.csv}, frozen
\texttt{protocol.json} and manifest still
require separate GitHub archival verification.

Under the \emph{illustrative}, not physically
derived, upper paired-CI margin of $+0.5$
percentage points versus same-response,
same-seed FP64, FP6 has a qualifying
upper bound of $+0.4824$ pp, but the
three other tested low-bit formats remain
\emph{inconclusive}. Specifically, an
uncertainty interval crossing $+0.5$ pp
does not establish inferior quality.
At the alternative exploratory $+1.0$
pp margin, FP5, FP6 and INT8 pass this
descriptive screening calculation while
INT6 remains inconclusive.
The $+0.5$ result for FP6 is near the
threshold and must not be interpreted
as a confirmatory pass after
retrospective width/graph selection.
The frozen M5 historical screens are
unchanged.

\paragraph{Interpretation and limitation.}
INT8 has much lower raw coefficient MSE
than FP6, yet not a lower \emph{observed
mean} Max-Cut gap in this small viewed-graph
study. Neither source coefficient error nor
the fraction of zeroed low-magnitude
coefficients fully determines the
state-dependent local-field error or
the stochastic trajectory. This
descriptive mismatch is a motivation
to assess quality directly, not
a statistical proof that FP6 dominates
INT8. The exact direct FP5-versus-FP6
and INT8-versus-FP6 paired uncertainty
is stored in the new EX1
\texttt{exploratory\_summary.csv};
the console excerpt used for
the first transcription printed only
comparisons against FP64, so
no direct-format CI values are
inferred here.

EX1 deliberately holds the graph, solver
and ideal device response fixed.
It does not determine $b^*_{\mathrm{FP}}$,
$b^*_{\mathrm{INT}}$, the sign of
$k=b^*_{\mathrm{INT}}-b^*_{\mathrm{FP}}$,
or a gain/offset interaction.
The next credible precision claim
requires a separate prospectively
selected, genuinely unviewed graph
split and confidence/multiplicity
plan, with an explicit finite
seed cap and an inconclusive outcome
when the evidence does not resolve
the boundary. No INT7 or follow-on
seed top-up was launched here.
