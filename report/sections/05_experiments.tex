\section{Bounded experiment programme}

The immediate sequence is E00 engineering verification, E01 real-trace structure and
E02 target physics. Their outcome and full closest-work inspection select one main
intervention and one backup. The programme does not launch all families as large campaigns.
Quality, latency and area budgets are frozen before each scientific comparison; no
unjustified numerical acceptance target is introduced here.

\subsection{First executable workload screen}

E01 accepts a real trace bundle with binary matrices $X_{\mathrm{cal}}$, $X_{\mathrm{eval}}$
of width 256, one $256\times256$ weight plane and disjoint stable IDs. Model/quantization
hashes, the layer, exporter command and data role are recorded. Padding and unused
columns are explicit. Exact matrix output is checked for every mapping.
One resident row order must serve all activation planes and inputs sharing the programmed
array; pooled calibration traces fit that order. Per-plane optima are diagnostic oracles
unless their reprogramming or routing costs are included.

The comparator set uses the calibration traces only. It reports occupancy histograms,
nonempty vector-read counts, total active rows and conditional fan-in at prospective
group sizes 16, 32, 64, 128 and 256. A squared-activity statistic is diagnostic; no physical
error or energy law is assigned to it. Hardware and inference metrics remain unavailable.
The largest skip count is not declared a winning research method.

\subsection{First bounded circuit set}

E02 first fixes one topology and nominal operating point. After checking current/bias
limits, nominate two supported parallelism settings and 12--24 nominal pattern cases
spanning zero, sparse, half-active and dense masks, equal-count physical placements and
contrasting stored states. The range is a planning budget, not an executed case count.
Check source extraction, KCL, driver headroom, current versus count and settling. Use
additional independent patterns to validate a reduced model, especially near decoding
edges. Introduce persistent and repeat-read variation in separate arms afterwards.

The preflight currently reports missing model fields. No unverified transistor port
ordering, target netlist generator or new Spectre execution is claimed by this reset.
The target model must be connected to actual authorized circuit sources before running.

\subsection{E03Q: finite-ADC decoder sensitivity}

Before claiming a covariance-aware mapping advantage, specify the entire sensed-current
transfer: input quantity and units, ADC full-scale and endpoints, bit depth, midrise or
midtread code convention, reference calibration, saturation and clipping, and ties.
Use nearest-\emph{quantized}-reference decoding rather than treating an ADC name as
a complete electrical specification. Compare ideal midpoint decoding, fixed finite
conversion and equally budgeted reference/full-scale tuning with persistent chip
identities paired across both mappings.

The constructed E03M fixture permits a diagnostic software-only E03Q extension while
the actual E02 circuit remains unresolved. Its nominal active read has 16 selected
cells with eight low- and eight high-current MTJs; current units are dimensionless.
The pilot assumes a midrise ADC over [16,32], 4--7 output bits, and offsets
$-0.25,0,+0.25$ LSB. Calibration selects a phase using independent chip assignments;
the evaluation set is held out. These settings are explicitly \emph{illustrative}, not
GF 22FDX reference, ADC or timing specifications.

Test matched read counts, marginal error laws, decoded pair covariance and reconstructed
MSE; separately report signed-error tails. If marginal laws are not demonstrably matched,
the MSE gap cannot be attributed solely to covariance. Full task evaluation must
add fixed-random, equal-budget best-of-random, shuffling, CAR, balancing, adaptive-block
and calibrated sensing baselines. A reduced MSE is a first screening result, not an
inference-failure or hardware-cost claim.

\subsection{Fair comparisons and stopping}

The revision-15 follow-up divides Ising screening into E09A exact software controls,
E09B conditional-error and cache-drift checks, and E09C a bounded solver pilot after a
validated target adapter. E09A is complete; E09B/C are unrun. The first proposed pilot
budget is three small graphs with enumerated optima, three independent chip assignments
and twenty RNG restarts per graph/chip. These are effort limits, not a powered result.
Temporal noise needs separate target provenance. A redraw of manufacturing variation
at each read is a diagnostic counterfactual and is not automatically physical read noise.

Digital incremental updates are a mandatory baseline. Include initialization, coupling
fetches, any transposed/duplicated storage, cache precision and refresh. Match full
conditional error laws on independent fixed states before interpreting adaptive
trajectories. Score the true target Hamiltonian and keep graph/chip/restart units
separate. Without hardware timing, report operation budgets rather than measured TTS.

Each baseline receives the same calibration/tuning budget and may adjust supported
references, ADC settings and sample time. Mark adaptations honestly where full circuit
details are inaccessible. Preserve chips and input assignments across paired policies.
Count correction and repeated-read overhead. An illustrative model can identify a
mechanism under assumptions, but cannot substitute for target validation.

A failed candidate is retained in the decision record with its reason. A novel useful
analytical rule may independently qualify as a contribution; a baseline winning a plot
does not automatically become such a rule. Expand evaluation after a strong small
comparison passes.
