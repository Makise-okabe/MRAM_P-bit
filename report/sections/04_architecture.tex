\section{Target architecture and model contract}

The target capacity is fixed, while read parallelism, scheduling and isolation are
unresolved. A logical batch is a schedule within the physical array; it does not remove
wire capacitance or create independent short bitlines. Physical sectors require explicit
isolation devices and local/global sensing choices, with resistance, capacitance, area
and timing overhead.

Initial parameterized models may use defensible ranges and reduced equivalents with
explicit limitations. Quantitative final claims need validated models and, where
appropriate, extracted parasitics. Neighboring-column and shared-driver effects must
either be explicit or justified by an equivalent loading representation.

The source boundary permits authorized access-device use in the university environment
with the student-owned MTJ model; it does not authorize redistributing licensed models.
No proprietary foundry MTJ implementation is bundled. Actual Spectre execution and
source validation take place in the authorized environment.

\subsection{Cost and statistical contract}

Total cost includes array and driver excitation, sensing/ADC, digital accumulation,
control/data movement and leakage. Repeated conversions, rejected speculation,
permutation addressing, metadata, fallback and calibration amortization are included.
Useful output precision and throughput must match across analog and digital alternatives.

Mappings and settings are fitted on calibration data, then frozen. Previously inspected
datasets remain development data. A device mismatch assignment persists across reads;
repeated reads are not independent chips. Report ground-truth classification error,
clean-reference mismatch and arithmetic error separately. Paired policies share input
and chip realizations, and their uncertainty must respect that pairing.
